Privacy Statement

Last updated: May 31, 2026

This privacy statement explains how LaBaNi 2026 handles guest information used for ticketing, QR access, zone upgrades and event entry.

Information We Collect

We may collect guest name, phone number, ticket number, selected zones, VIP status, amount paid, ticket issue time, upgrade records and gate or zone scan results.

How We Use Information

We use guest information to issue tickets, verify payments, generate QR passes, manage zone access, prevent ticket fraud, support event operations and provide guest support.

Local Storage

The app may store ticket and scan data in the browser's local storage on the device being used. This helps the app display tickets, manage QR verification and support offline-style access during event operations.

Sharing

Guest information may be shared with event organizers, venue staff, security personnel, payment support contacts or service providers where needed for event access, safety, verification or support.

Security

We use practical safeguards to protect ticket and access information, but no digital system is completely risk-free. Guests should keep ticket QR codes private and avoid sharing screenshots with others.

Retention

Ticket and event access records may be kept for event administration, dispute resolution, security review and legal compliance, then removed when no longer needed.

Contact

For privacy questions or correction requests, contact the Last Born Association of Nigeria through the official LaBaNi channels.
